me

opinions are my own. i'll only be here on this earth for so long and i've found my time to be most useful when i'm being curious/learning about things, building things, and helping other people. i don't think you should work a job, i think you should work on projects and the nuance is that you shouldn’t hack away at things with no purpose. the goal of the project should be to build something then move onto the next. now i just want to clarify working at a job is perfectly fine, just don’t work the job. like Paul Graham advises strive to build cool new things.

i really like Tom Hougaards book called “best looser wins”. the concept that if you’re willing to be naive enough to fail and persist, when you win you win big aka retardmaxxing. to illustrate if you know “ready player one” he only wins by going full throttle in reverse which to be honest sounds insane.

i try really hard to be good at what i do. if not what’s the purpose? i’m wasting both my time and yours if not.
